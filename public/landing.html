Everything you need

Built for
serious revision.

MCQ + Theory
Up to 60 multiple choice questions and 10 theory questions per quiz. Both question types, fully graded, in one session.
100% Private
Your PDF is extracted entirely in your browser using pdf.js. The raw content of your slides is never uploaded to any server.
Quiz History
Every quiz you complete is saved to your account. Review all questions, your answers, and model answers side by side, any time.
Export Results
Download any quiz as a PDF or Word document. Full questions, your answers, correct answers, and model answers — all formatted cleanly.
Re-download Slides
Every uploaded PDF is saved locally in your browser. Download it back at any time directly from your recents list.
Instant Feedback
After every answer, your chosen lecturer gives you personalised feedback in their voice — harsh, warm, smug, or encouraging.
